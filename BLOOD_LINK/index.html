About BLOOD LINK

BLOOD LINK is a technology-enabled voluntary coordination platform connecting willing blood donors, families in emergency need, and verified healthcare facilities.

Core Safety & Legal Principles:

  • Non-Commercial: Blood donation is strictly voluntary and altruistic. Commercial trade or monetization of blood products is illegal and prohibited.
  • Clinical Integrity: Blood Link facilitates digital contact. Blood cross-matching, infectious disease screening, and all clinical procedures happen exclusively at certified hospital blood centers.
  • Privacy Protection: Phone numbers are never exposed to public crawlers or unverified third parties.
